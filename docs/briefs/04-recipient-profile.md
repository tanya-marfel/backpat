# 04 - Recipient profile

## Purpose
Adapt framing and tone to the person being written about, especially for feedback they will read directly.

## Requirements
- `people/<slug>/profile.md` captures:
  - Role, level, team, relationship to the author.
  - Feedback preference: direct, balanced, or gentle. Private versus public praise.
  - Motivators and goals (for example promotion, a skill they are building).
  - Sensitivities to avoid.
  - Competency framework or career ladder in use, if any.
- Filled in from:
  - A short interview with the author.
  - Optionally, the recipient answers a self-questionnaire.
  - Inferences from sources, each marked as `inferred` with a citation and confirmed by the author.
- The writer uses the profile for tone and emphasis only. It must never invent achievements from it.

## Open questions
- Support a shareable self-questionnaire link in the app stage? Default: later.

## Acceptance criteria
- The same evidence plus different recipient profiles produce visibly different framing, with identical facts.
- Inferred traits are never written into a profile without the author confirming them.
